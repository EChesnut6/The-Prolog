The Pre-View

What to know before watching

The Premise

Highschooler Sam Witwicky is an average Chicago kid living in the suburubs with a quick wit. He's not too popular at school and mostly forgotten about. His only claim to fame is his great grandfather's discovery at the south pole (I think it's the south pole but im not certain). He's finally getting a car, an old beat up something idek. BUT WAIT ITS A FUCKING ROBOT!?!?!?! Any who, Sam with his quick wit and a crazy good wigman helps him bag Mikaela. Then more robots show up and stuff happens. Also the military is doing some stuff too.

Essentials

Look this movie is not that serious, but it's really funny and a lot of fun. You could get by not paying full attention, but it's fun enough I would suggest you do. I mention Sam's quick wit and that's really the comedy of this whole movie, it's fast, so if you don't pay attention you'll miss it. But as I alluded to this isn't a movie that you should be worried about being spoiled, so if you haven't seen it somehow feel free to watch the full trailer.

Why you should watch it

I haven't written a section like this for any other movies, but I feel it's important for this one, and others I haven't written yet.

Having good taste means tasting many things. I once heard someone compare the films, and entertainment in general, you watch to a diet. In order to have a good one you have to have the right mix of stuff. That means you have to have your veggies, your protein, and a little junk food and sweets too. You can probably guess which Transformers is. But as far as junk food goes, I'd say it's a pretty good one, like a coffee ice cream or maybe a s'more, sorry I'm really hungry. Transformers has a little bit of everything that makes a great blockbuster epic. It's exciting, sexy, funny, and just downright awesome. If you're reading this I'd probably assume I forced you to because you're my friend or family, or I finally posted the site on reddit and you're a movie fan. That being so, it's good to understand Bay and his style because like it or not he is/was huge and regardless of if you want to admit it but he will/has influenced movies forever, and that is worthy of being studied.

"I make movies for teenage boys. Oh, dear, what a crime." — Michael Bay

Is Transformers a great magnificent cinematic masterpiece? Of course not, and it isn't trying to be that. Michael Bay and his movies have plenty of faults, but he knows what he makes and he does it how he wants to, and sometimes they're really entertaining.

Transformers Fans

This was my first introduction into the world of Transformers, and I know that the fandom is probably rolling their eyes. I don't know how this is compared to a lot of the other media or lore, but like it or not, this movie helped the franchise by introducing it to so many people and a whole new generation. I can say compared to the only other piece of Transformers media I've seen, Transformers One, this is not the best, but it's the most entertaining.
