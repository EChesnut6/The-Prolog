The Other Guys

2010 • Directed by Adam McKay

“NY's finest were busy.”

The Pre-View

What to know before watching

The Premise

After "The Guys" (the big shot cops) at the NYPD meet their untimely demise, two unlikely partners stumble into a massive conspiracy with billions of dollars on the line.

Essentials

I do not recommend watching the trailer. I'll give some explanation of the type of comedy, but with comedies, you watch it, you see it, you stop caring. If you watch a comedy trailer, they are usually going to show the best jokes, so don't do it. Mark Wahlberg and Will Ferrel absolutely kill it, and the rest of the cast do fantastic work. This movie was made by the same people who made Taledaga Nights and Step Brothers. I also wouldn't be surprised if a lot of the jokes were improvised. With all of the talented cast, the bloopers are probably funnier than most of the movies coming out today.

The Comedy Scene

This movie came out near the end of an era of comedy in Hollywood. I'll need to research more about the best comedies to come out after, but I think there is a strong correlation with the rise of PC movement and the decline in great comedies. Off the top of my head something like The Nice Guys is one of the more recent great comedies, and I don't know about what else but stuff like Meet the Parents and Wedding Crashers were some around the same time that were great. I think after that you get a lot more romcom style movies.

The Transition

Post 2000s comedy were highly centered around raunchy and crude humor. This was a result of the general cultural movement in response to things such as the rise of the internet, the post Y2K era, and mid Great Recession era. To cope with so many high anxiety-inducing events, the comedy shifted towards darker forms of comedy. It was a time that focused on emotionally messy, grounded, desparate, and usually sex-filled comedy. This then transitioned into the meme era with highly meta and less obscene comedy. It is self referential, and focused on clever witty humor. This movie falls right in the middle where it has a mix of the crass humor of the 00s and the clever wit of the 10s. I think this is a reason the comedy hits so perfectly. It was both in tune with the era but also ahead of it's time.
