The Primer

What to know before watching

You can go into this movie knowing nothing or everything, it's still confusing. howerver, to make your viewing mor interesting its important to point out some references upfront.

There are key visual references to many pueces of aet including Hypnosis by schrodinger??? and that painting of Prometheus. the move has many references to greek mythology and the odessy, having some familiarity with some of the lore might be helpful. specifically, the character of proteus, a shapeshifter who must be grappled with and conquered. you will also note the great question and importance of what is true. to steal from a post on reddit, grappling with proteus is an allegory for grappling with truth.

the mood of the movie is incredibly surreal, tense
