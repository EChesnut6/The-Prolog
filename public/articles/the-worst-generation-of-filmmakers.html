The Worst Generation of Filmmakers

By Eli Chesnut • 2026-07-02

Generations of Film

Every generation has their standout filmmakers. The baby boomers have truly great artists such as ... Gen X maybe took a step back in general except for this greats ... And then the millenials have ... And maybe this is all just time, we haven't given enough time for millenials to get in their zone, but they might be out of time already. This year we have received shockingly fantastic horror films by Gen Z directors. This might be a fluke but it could also be a sign that times are changing and the new generation has arrived early. Growing up entirely with the internet and more importantly youtube and social media gave the younger generation a push towards embracing creation earlier than normal and on a larger scale. Being able to share productions right away led to early lessons and critics. This lead to artists refining their craft earlier. Millenials might be disappointed again after attempting to follow the traditional path. By waiting their turn and GenZ getting active in creating content online early, they may not be ready and refined enough. Especially as their humor may have phased out on the modern movie-going audience. While GenZ was the youngest when streaming became the norm, studies show they are actually the biggest movie theater going audience now, which may mean the types of movies that millenias want to make may already be out of fashion, because like it or not money talks.
