## Primer

#### The Premise
Guy likes girl. Girl likes guy??? Guy makes a wish that irreparably changes his and everyone around him's lives. Typical love story. 

#### The Essentials
We've been here before. You think you've already seen this movie, but this takes it to a different level. I'm writing this while it's still in theaters, but I don't know when I'll update this again, so I wanna go ahead and call my shot. Inde Navarette is going to become an absolute superstar. Not trynna act crazy, but she's really hot. From interviews I've watched she comes off as genuine and sweet. This is her first big movie and she gives such an incredible performance. Unfortunately its a horror movie and she has somewhat limited range due to the circumstances, but she 100% deserves a nomination for best actress. I wouldn't be surprised if the Deadmeat awards give it to her, she definitely deserves it. At this point I'm kinda rambling, so I'll need to organize after. Going in I can't think of much you should know. This guy has a crush on his somewhat long time friend and wants to tell her how he feels, but due to the sheer terror that is telling someone how you feel, he doesn't. He then uses a One Wish Willow asking for her to love him more than anyone in the world. And surprise surprise, it works, but not as intended. His name is Baron, but everyone calls him Bear. This could be a reference to the trend where women online would say they'd rather be in the woods alone with a bear than with a man. Bear's best friend is Ian who kinda flip flops on how/when Bear should tell Nikki. The girl's name is Nikki. There's also Sarah who has a crush on Bear. It's not greatly explained how the friend group came to be, but they all work at the same music store owned by Sarah's dad. I'm not sure if I really need to say all this, but this is a horror movie, so it's got jump scares, gore, and is kinda scary. The vibe is like not super happy or anything, but idk why anyone would think it is. It's fun, I think it is at least, but like not fun for people who don't like horror movies.
