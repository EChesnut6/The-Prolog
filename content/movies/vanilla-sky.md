## Primer

I'll mention that this is actually a remake of the spanish movie titled "Open your eyes." However, the director Cameron Crowe considers his version more of a remix rather than a true remake. The director of the spanish version, Alejandro Amenábar, also considers them to be the same song sung by very different voices, one more operatic while the other is rock and roll. Another fun fact is that Penelope Cruz plays the same character, Sofia, in both. Does that make a big difference when watching? No. Is it interesting? Yes. 

As for things you should know, this movie has a little sci-fi going on and is purposefully confusing. You will and should ask what is real while watching. The movie often jumps forwards and backwards in time showing glimpses of whats to come. For watching the [trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k09OX40NLUw) I would stop at 1:22. From this the essential plot that you should be aware of is clear, David (Tom Cruise) is a young wealthy guy with few connections to anyone. He has a casual relationship with Julie (Cameron Diaz), has a lot of money, nice apartment, and even a fancy job. However, it then gets flipped on his head when he meets Sofia (Penelope Cruz). There is much more to it than that, but I think this is the essential plot that you should be aware of going in.
