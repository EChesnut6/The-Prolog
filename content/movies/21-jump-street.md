#### The Essentials
As with most comedies, I'd argue to not watch the trailer at all because they normally spoil the best jokes anyways. Of course, if you don't see any of the jokes how will you know if you will think it's funny? Great question and one I don't have an answer to. Just watch the fucking movie.

#### The Original
Not an original concept and there are self references to it throughout. The original was a cop drama with essentially the same premise. 4 young cops get sent back to high school to stop crime. The show lasted for 5 seasons and was a major success for the new up and coming Fox network, also giving a spark to young actor Johnny Depp.
