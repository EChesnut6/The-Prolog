## Review

Watched this movie after remembering something about Darron Aranofsky "stealing" or "plagarising" *Black Swan* entirely from this movie. I remember originally thinking, wow reall? He stole the ENTIRE thing? So I watched this. It's been a while since I saw *Black Swan* but obviously with that film in the back of my mind while watching this, it affected how I saw the movie. However, ultimately I just don't know how anyone could walk away from watch both and try to call Aranofsky a thief. His idea feels completely original and separate. I think what he mostly gets flack for is that he denies being influenced. However here are his exact words on the subject,

> "Not really, there are similarities between the films, but it wasn’t influenced by it. It really came out of Swan Lake the Ballet, we wanted to dramatize the ballet, that’s why it’s kind of up here and down there, because ballet is big and small in lots of ways."
> — Aranofsky

I think from this it's clear he interpereted being influenced as deciding to create the script/story from this film. Aparently, he has also noted Satoshi Kon as an influential director before. I'll finish by saying I think Aranofsky is in the clear and doesn't deserve any hate. There are clearly similarites between the movies in story and style, but they are ultimately about different things and are done in different ways.

So anyways, I think the movie is great. It does a fantastic job of keeping you lost and confused while also giving just enough to let the individual come to their own understanding.
