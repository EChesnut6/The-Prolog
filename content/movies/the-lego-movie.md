## Primer

This movie is so much fucking fun. So many cool small details you could point out. For example, Jonnah Hill voices the Green Lantern and Channing Tatum voices Superman. Keep in mind this movie came out the same year as 22 Jump Street and 2 years after 21 Jump Street where they both co-star. The casting is 1000% on purpose and adds an extra layer of humor if you know this. The cast is insanely stacked, and while it's fair to not take this movie seriously, you are rewarded for doing so. This movie is fucking awesome.
