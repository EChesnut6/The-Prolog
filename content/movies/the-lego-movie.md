## Primer

#### The Premise
A normal everyday guy, Emmet, that is special in no way at all ends up falling, literally, into a global conspiracy where he must realize his full potential and help the Master Builders fight back and stop President Business from using his secret weapon. 

#### Essentials
Unironically, this is a great movie that is just so insanely fun. Yes you can easily just throw this on in the background and pay 30% attention and you'll be cheaply entertained, but if you give this movie a legitimate watch and pay attention, you'll be greatly rewarded. This movie has so many incredible details and small jokes that make it so much more hilarious and entertaining. You'll also get a deeper appreciation of the larger message of the movie as a whole, which is incredibly heartfelt and heart warming. For the [trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fZ_JOBCLF-I), I honeslty can say you should watch the entire thing, so no worries about important spoilers other than a handful of the many brilliant jokes being repeated.

#### Small Things
I mention the small details, and they can be very small. For example, Jonnah Hill voices the Green Lantern and Channing Tatum voices Superman. Keep in mind this movie came out the same year as [[22 Jump Street]] and 2 years after [[21 Jump Street]] where they both co-star. The casting is 1000% on purpose and adds an extra layer of humor if you know this. In general you could talk about how insane the cast is in this movie. Nearly every character big and small is voiced by a major celebrity. 

#### Technical Wonder
Something awesome about this movie is the painstaking effort that was put in to develop its style. There had been lego movies before, but those were more animated movies that used LEGO-like characters and sets. This movie set out to redefine that style. You'll notice that everything is lego pieces; the smoke, lasers, fires, all of it. It was all intentionaly designed and made so that it looks like a stop motion lego movie. Besides everything looking like LEGO, they also were intentinoaly about it feeling like LEGO. The way the characters move is all accurate and even small details like fingerprints being visible on the pieces can be seen in the movie. It's truly a fantastic piece of technical effort to bring this to life, so it should be appreciated for that aspect.

## Review

#### It is Everything
I mean lets be honest. This movie is everything and everything is awesome! Maybe it's the nestolgia, but this movie is just so much fun!!! Is there any individual thing about this movie that makes it stand out so much and makes it so much fun? Not that I can think of. This movie thrives on simplicity. The plot is simple. The comedy is simple. The acting is pretty simple. I think it just comes down to everything working together so well. The joyful and exciting narrative that comes from the mind of a child is just so fantastic. I find this heartwarming father-son story to hit home perfectly. And this movie is kinda crazy deep in that way if you explore it. This young kid has so much creativity and frustration with his dad while also loving him and just wanting his approval. It's something that essentially everyone can relate to. The father realizing he's made grave mistakes with the relationship he has with his son and making the change is so sweet and just makes you feel happy. The movie also has some kinda crazy and not so subtle critiques on capitalism and society. The movie points out the corruption within our systems that allow for a few people to govern and control so much of what happens in our world. It also points out the strange sameness that evolved out of the American individualistic capitalist system. Emmet, being the "ideal" citizen who likes all of the most popular things, thus being devoid of any real since of individuality, until he breaks out of that system. However, being a kids movie it doesn't go too in depth, but it also shouldn't because thats not what this movie is about. This movie is in fact a really expensive ad for LEGO, but a really well done ad at that. LEGO does a good job of showcasing all of their different sets that can be built and left alone and generic, but also shows what the company is all about, creativity. Originally LEGO wasn't sold as sets but just as blocks that you were meant to let your imagination run wild and build whatever you wanted. This review is pretty chaotic and not organized so I will need to come back to it and work on it more, but this is decent for now.
