#### Essentials
This American guy named Richard (aka Rick) owns a really cool bar in Casablanca in the year 1941. World War II is going wild and French Morocco is a weird limbo state that isn't totally free and independent but slightly more so than European France. Rick is known as a bit of a neutralist cynic who doesn't seem to care about much. The local french sheriff dude fucks with him cause he gets to win at roulette in the backroom where illegal gambling is rampant.
